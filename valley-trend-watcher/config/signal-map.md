# Signal Map — moment to Valley product

What the watcher uses to turn "they built a cabin" into "here's the product and
here's the post". Sourced from the Valley Products Master and the Glossary (The
Translator).

Keep this honest. A tie-in that sends someone to a product we can't supply costs
more than saying nothing.

Status key matches the Glossary: **STOCK** we hold it · **SUBSTITUTE** does the
same job · **MACHINE** we could mill it to order · **GAP** no Valley answer.

## Weather — the fastest signal

An orange or red Met Office wind warning covering the South West means fencing
demand within days, not weeks. Treat it as outranking anything on television.

When a warning lands:
- Post before the storm, not after. "Check your posts before Friday" is more
  useful and more shareable than "we sell fence posts".
- Have stock and a straight answer on lead time ready.
- Named storms get their own news cycle. That is free reach in our catchment.

Feed: <https://metoffice.github.io/nswws-public-api/> (free, machine-readable)

## Hard rules for any suggested post

1. **No prices in copy.** House rule from the product master. Prices go stale.
2. **No em-dashes.** House style.
3. **Never tie in on a GAP.** Pivot to the nearest SUBSTITUTE and say so plainly,
   or skip it.
4. **Use the customer's words.** The Glossary's "also known as" column exists for
   this. People search "sterling board", not "OSB3".
5. **Never imply a programme endorsed us.** Reference the look, the technique or
   the material.
6. **Respect the season.** A brilliant decking post in December is a save-for-
   spring asset. Say so rather than burning it.
7. **Local first.** If the moment has a South West angle, lead with it.
