and here's the post". Sourced from the Valley Products Master and the
Glossary (The Translator). Keep this honest: if we don't hold it, say so —
a tie-in that sends someone to a product we can't supply costs more than
saying nothing.

## Business context the watcher should assume
- **Who:** Valley Sawmills (Herriard Sawmills Ltd), Holwell, Nr Nunney, Frome, BA11 4PZ.
- **Catchment:** Somerset / Wiltshire / Dorset, plus national interest in slabs and specials.
- **Mix:** trade and retail. Trade wants spec and availability; retail wants the look.
- **Differentiator:** we are a working sawmill, not a shed. We can mill to order,
  we have slabs and specials nobody else locally holds. Lead with that.
- **Channels:** Instagram and Facebook. Site: valleysawmills.co.uk

## Hard rules for any suggested post
1. **No prices in copy.** House rule from the product master — prices go stale.
2. **No em-dashes.** House style.
3. **Never tie in on a GAP.** If the screen moment maps to something we don't
   hold, either pivot to the nearest SUBSTITUTE and say so plainly, or skip it.
4. **Use the customer's words**, not ours. The Glossary's "also known as"
   column exists for exactly this — people search "sterling board", not "OSB3".
5. **Don't claim a show endorsed us.** Reference the look, the technique or the
   material, never imply a partnership.
