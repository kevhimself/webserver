# How Valley Actually Posts

From Kev and Caspar's social conversation, 29 September 2026, and the firewood
social plan. **The watcher writes to fit this, not the other way round.**

## Facebook first. This is the big one.

The watcher used to draft Instagram-shaped posts. That was wrong.

| | Followers | Audience | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Facebook** | 111 (Sept 2026) | Local, mostly men, biggest group 55 to 64 then 45 to 54 | **Where our buyers already are.** Lead here. |
| **Instagram** | 504 | Mostly men 35 to 44, only about 43% UK | Second stop. Costs nothing extra. Don't chase the number. |

**Facebook first, Instagram later the same day.** Hashtags are an Instagram
habit: a Facebook post does not need a row of them. Write the Facebook version
first and let Instagram take the same words.

The strategist's warning is worth repeating: we could hit 750 Instagram
followers by attracting people who will never order. Kev's steer is **"not the
big numbers, the right numbers"**.

## The three pillars

Every post maps to one. **The watcher must say which.**

1. **Proof it's reliable and ready** — the wood is good and it's ready now.
   Moisture meter checks, seasoned versus kiln dried, drying sheds, log art.
2. **Trust and story** — who we are and where it comes from. Locally sourced,
   Len, Garth, 50 years.
3. **Ease of buying** — how simple it is to get it. Garry's deliveries, what's
   in the bag, collection, forklifts moving timber.

**Weighting:** roughly two posts a week on proof and ease, which convert now, and
one on trust and story to keep the relationship warm.

## When

- **Three to four posts a week** across Kev and Caspar combined.
- **Monday or Tuesday:** trust and story. Doesn't need to chase a weekend
  decision, so it suits the start of the week.
- **Thursday and Friday:** prime people right before the weekend, when they
  actually buy.
- **Sunday evening:** the wind-down, thinking-about-the-fire moment.
- **Wednesday evening** is being tested, because general research puts Facebook's
  peak midweek. Our own weekend pattern wins unless the test says otherwise.
- **Time of day: 5 to 7pm.**
- **Scheduled** through Meta Business Suite, banked in advance.

**Monday is the weekly review.** Which is exactly when the brief lands, so the
brief should be usable in that meeting without rework.

## Who

**Caspar owns firewood social end to end.** Kev posts too, in step. The rule they
set is that the two voices read as one: same pillars whoever is posting, shared
tone, and a quick heads-up between them before posting.

So the watcher's job is to hand them something they can post, not to decide what
gets posted.

## What actually works

**Raw, real footage beats the polished version with music.** Firewood coming off
the lorry outperformed the produced test. Real people, real timber, movement.
Garry, Garth and Len at work.

**Use sparingly:** plain timber-in-a-yard or logs-in-a-fire shots. Every
competitor posts those. Backdrop, not the whole post.

## The two rings

These are different and the watcher must not confuse them.

- **20 miles around Frome** for social campaigns and Google Ads. The firewood
  buyer. This is the tighter, commercial ring.
- **Frome plus 60 miles** for the general catchment, and further for slabs and
  specials, which we ship.

Firewood and boundaries content aims at 20 miles. Cladding, slabs and structural
can reach wider.

## Where the watcher plugs in

The October campaign list already includes **"Before the cold"**: weather watcher
posts, Facebook first, saying a cold or windy spell is coming, here's what's in
stock, here's when the delivery runs are. Helpful, never alarming.

**That is exactly the Met Office signal the watcher carries.** When a wind
warning lands over the South West, the watcher is feeding an agreed campaign, not
inventing one. Say so in the brief, so whoever is posting knows it's already
signed off in principle.

## October 2026 targets, for context

Facebook 111 to 200 followers. Instagram 504 to 750. Reach up 25%. Online sales
£6,000, against £3,590 in September. Tracked every Monday.

The strategist's view, worth holding onto: the outcomes that actually matter are
**firewood orders from local postcodes** and **Facebook visits and followers**.
