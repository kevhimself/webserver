# The Watchlist

Reweighted for **mainstream reach**. A two-minute garden item on The One Show
reaches more of our actual customers than a whole episode of a specialist build
show. Specialist shows still matter because the intent is higher, but they come
second.

## Tier 1 — mainstream reach
Big audiences, ordinary viewers, our catchment watches these.

| Show | Channel | Slot | Why it matters to us |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The One Show | BBC One | Weeknights 7pm | Huge reach, regular garden and DIY items. A single slot can move a product. |
| Countryfile | BBC One | Sun evening | Woodland, forestry, rural building. Our story lives here. Best fit of any show on TV for a working sawmill. |
| Morning Live | BBC One | Weekday mornings | Daytime DIY and garden segments, very mainstream, often practical and seasonal. |
| DIY SOS: The Big Build | BBC One | Primetime | Decking, ramps, fencing, structural. Big emotional reach. |
| The Repair Shop | BBC One | Primetime | Wood craft, oak, reclaimed. Soft halo, big audience, strong for slab and craft content. |
| Escape to the Country | BBC One | Daytime | Rural property, outbuildings, barns. Our catchment exactly. |
| Homes Under the Hammer | BBC One | Daytime | Renovation intent, trade audience. |
| Love Your Garden | ITV1 | Summer run | Sleepers, decking, pergolas. The mainstream garden build show. |

## Local angle — Frome plus 60 miles
A national moment we can't serve is worth less than a regional one we can. The
watcher should weight anything filmed in, set in, or reported from:

**Somerset · Wiltshire · Dorset · Bath and North East Somerset · Bristol · South
Gloucestershire · west Hampshire**, and over the bridge to Newport and Cardiff.

Towns inside the radius include Bath, Bristol, Wells, Glastonbury, Shepton
Mallet, Warminster, Trowbridge, Westbury, Devizes, Chippenham, Salisbury,
Yeovil, Taunton, Bridgwater, Weston-super-Mare, Dorchester, Swindon, Marlborough,
Bournemouth, Poole, Winchester, Gloucester and Cheltenham.

A Grand Designs filmed in Somerset is a completely different proposition from one
in Aberdeen. Say so when it happens.

## Notes on the Ground Force effect
A show creates a look, viewers search the look, they buy the materials one to
three weeks later. Ground Force did it for decking in the late 90s hard enough to
reshape the garden trade.

The advantage is in the **pre-air** column, and in the **season**. A decking
moment in April is worth ten of the same moment in November. Read this file
alongside `seasonal-calendar.md`, which outranks it.
