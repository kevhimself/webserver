## Notes on the Ground Force effect
The pattern we're trading on: a show creates a look, viewers search the look,
they buy the materials 1–3 weeks later. Ground Force did it for decking in the
late 90s so hard it reshaped the whole garden trade.

The advantage is in the **pre-air** column. If we know next Wednesday's Grand
Designs features larch cladding, we can be posting about larch cladding on
Wednesday night while everyone else finds out on Thursday.
