# The Seasonal Calendar

**This is the backbone. TV and social only modulate it.**

Demand at Valley is seasonal before it is anything else. A brief that leads with
"Grand Designs is on Wednesday" and ignores that it's the second week of October
has the priority the wrong way round. The watcher leads with the season, then
asks what on screen amplifies it.

## The year-round spine
These sell every month. They are not seasonal, so a screen moment is a genuine
uplift rather than a nudge to something already happening.

- **Construction timber** — beams, joists, structural sections
- **Boards for tables** — oak and Douglas fir slabs, live edge
- **Cladding** — all species

## The four recurring engines

**1. Firewood has two seasons, not one.**
Winter warmth is obvious. **Summer fire pits are the missed one** — people buying
firewood in June for an evening outside don't think of a timber merchant. That's an
open goal twice a year.

**2. Fencing follows weather, not the calendar.**
Autumn through spring is the baseline, but the real spikes are storms. A named
Met Office wind warning over the South West means fence panels and posts within
days. This is the fastest-moving signal we have and it is free to watch.

**3. Garden planning runs autumn and spring.**
Sleepers, raised beds and tree planting sit in both shoulders of the year, not
the summer. Autumn planning is the quieter and less contested of the two.

**4. Spring is a cliff, not a ramp.**
The shed, deck and garden furniture trade turns on in March almost overnight.
Content needs to be live *before* the first dry weekend, not in response to it.

## How the watcher should use this

1. **Start with the month.** What should be selling anyway?
2. **Then ask what's on screen** that amplifies it. A decking moment in April is
   worth ten times a decking moment in November.
3. **Flag counter-seasonal moments honestly.** If Grand Designs shows a stunning
   deck in December, say it's a save-for-spring asset rather than a post-now one.
4. **Never let TV override the season.** If it's the second week of October and
   nothing relevant is on, the brief is about firewood and fences. That is a
   complete and useful brief.
