Welcome.

You know you don't need fixing. You're not broken. But you might be feeling stuck, or overwhelmed at work, or unsure about your next steps. You may just have lost confidence in who you are.

I offer structured, thoughtful coaching to help you get clear on what matters, where you're stuck, and what to do about it. No jargon. No quick fixes. Just calm, honest conversations that turn overwhelm into small, achievable steps, with support that holds you to them.

I help people find clarity when work (and life) feels overwhelming. I work with you to untangle the confusion, reconnect with your strengths, and find practical next steps that feel authentically yours.

If you've got a problem, and you know where to find her, maybe you should hire… Ruth.

Let's talk.

What do I do?

Coaching is about authentic conversations, so rather than sweat over a neat summary, I had a chat about what I think my coaching is, and here's what I said:

"Coaching feels like a puzzle you're helping to solve with someone; helping them find out the answers, seeing people grow in confidence and clarity.

It's helping them realise their strengths, which they can apply to any situation they're struggling with, helping them join the dots.

Really, coaching is helping people see the potential that's already in them."

Who's it for?

Maybe you're thinking about a change: a new sector, a new role, a new way of working. But you're not sure how to get there, or even what "there" really looks like.

Or maybe it's not so dramatic. You're just feeling burnt out and disheartened, but can't quite articulate why.

How do I work?

My coaching is grounded, kind, curious and practical.

I meet you exactly where you are, with no one-size-fits-all approach. I create a calm space where you can think clearly and do your best work.

I listen deeply, help you find small, doable steps (not overwhelming transformations), and reflect back your own strengths, often ones you didn't know you had.

No jargon. No imposing methods. Just curious, caring support that leads to real, pragmatic steps forward.

Privacy & cookies

Last updated: October 2026

This notice explains what personal information I collect, why, and what you can ask me to do with it. I'm Ruth Blanco, a self-employed coach, and I'm responsible for your information (the "data controller"). You can contact me any time at ruthblanco1977@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • When you email me: your name, email address and whatever you choose to tell me.
  • When you book a call: bookings are handled by Calendly, which collects your name, email and any answers you give on the booking form, and shares them with me.
  • When we work together: contact details, notes from our sessions, and payment records.

Why I use it

  • To reply to you and arrange calls (legitimate interests, or steps you've asked for before a contract).
  • To deliver coaching you've signed up for (contract).
  • To keep financial records (legal obligation).

Coaching conversations can touch on personal topics. I keep session notes brief, store them securely, and never share them without your permission, unless the law requires it.

Who else sees it

Only the services I use to run my practice: Calendly (bookings), Zoom (online sessions) and Google (email). They process data on my behalf under their own privacy terms, and some of them store data outside the UK with legal safeguards in place. I never sell your information or use it for marketing lists.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to coaching: up to 12 months. Session notes: up to 2 years after our last session. Financial records: 6 years, as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or restrict how I use it. Just email me. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies, and doesn't set any cookies of its own. The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google to fetch them. When you click a booking button you go to Calendly's own site, where Calendly's cookie policy applies.
