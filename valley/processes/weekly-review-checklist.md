# Weekly Review Checklist

**When:** Every Monday morning (or pick your day)
**Time needed:** ~60-90 minutes
**Who:** Whoever owns product data that week

---

## Competitor Price Check (30-45 mins)

### Reference Products to Check

| # | Product | Check these competitors |
|---|---------|----------------------|
| 1 | 5'6" Featheredge Panel 6ft | Haven, Charltons, B&Q |
| 2 | New Treated Sleeper 2.4m | Haven, Charltons, Fountain Forestry, B&Q |
| 3 | Postcrete 20kg | B&Q, Amazon, Charltons |
| 4 | Fence Post 75x75mm 2.4m | Haven, Charltons, B&Q |
| 5 | Fence Post 100x100mm 3m | Haven, Charltons |
| 6 | Standard Decking Board 3.6m | B&Q, Charltons |
| 7 | Gravel Board 6ft | Haven, Charltons |
| 8 | Arris Rail 3.6m | Haven, Charltons |
| 9 | Trellis Panel 6x1ft | B&Q, Amazon |
| 10 | Fence Panel Caps | B&Q, Amazon |

### Process

1. Open competitor comparison template
2. Check each competitor's current price (website or call)
3. Update the template with new prices and today's date
4. Note the source (URL or "phone call")
5. Review any products where we're now >15% above cheapest

### Using AI to Help

**Prompt for competitor price checking:**
```
Please check the current prices for these products on [competitor website URL]:
1. Featheredge fence panel 6ft x 5'6"
2. New treated sleeper 2.4m
3. Postcrete 20kg
[etc]

For each, give me: product name as listed, price (inc VAT), any volume discounts noted, URL of product page.
```
